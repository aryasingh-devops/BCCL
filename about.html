About Us

Supporting BCCL employees and township communities

The portal helps employees access welfare services, township support, and grievance redressal from one place.

Our Mission

To provide responsive welfare services, transparent grievance handling, and reliable township management support for BCCL employees and their families in Dhanbad.

Our Vision

To build well-managed, inclusive, and service-oriented townships where employees can live with dignity, safety, and dependable civic support.

What We Manage

Welfare and township responsibilities

Employee First

Requests are organized around employee identity, department, township, and service category.

Transparent Records

Each grievance receives a database record that administrators can review and update.

Township Focus

Common issues such as water, sanitation, lighting, quarters, roads, and maintenance are clearly categorized.

Service Coordination

The admin page helps welfare staff see pending, in-progress, and resolved matters quickly.
